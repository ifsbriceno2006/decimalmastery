📍 Module 1 · Decimal place values

A decimal is a number with a decimal point. The point separates the whole number part (left) from the fraction part (right). Every place is 10 times bigger than the place to its right.

🎛️ Slide to build a decimal. The big square is 1 whole, cut into 100 small squares (hundredths).

🧱 Place value chart. Type any number:
⬅️ Moving left: × 10 for each place.
➡️ Moving right: ÷ 10 for each place.
🔴 The decimal point is not a place; it marks where the ones place ends.
🎯 Read 0.37 as “thirty-seven hundredths”, because the last digit is in the hundredths place.

➕ Module 4 · Add & subtract decimals

1️⃣ Line up the decimal points, one under the other.
2️⃣ Fill empty places with zeros.
3️⃣ Add or subtract like whole numbers.
4️⃣ Bring the decimal point straight down.

✖️ Module 5 · Multiply & divide decimals

✖️ Multiply
1️⃣ Ignore the decimal points.
2️⃣ Multiply as whole numbers.
3️⃣ Count the decimal places in both factors and add them.
4️⃣ Give the answer that many decimal places.
➗ Divide
1️⃣ Make the divisor a whole number: move its point right, and move the dividend’s point the same number of places.
2️⃣ Divide as usual.
3️⃣ The point in the answer goes straight above the point in the dividend.
